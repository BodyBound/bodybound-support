Blueprint Mode — Live Output

This is the new default production prompt. No flags, no pre-scan — every stencil generated through /api/ai-stencil and /api/process_stencil_job now follows Blueprint Mode.

Blueprint Mode rules:
  • Zero fills — no solid black, no gray, no filled regions anywhere. Every dark region in the reference is rendered as a contour.
  • Line-only output — lines describe structure, not shading.
  • 3-tier line weight hierarchy — PRIMARY (silhouette + major contours) / SECONDARY (internal features) / TERTIARY (texture + fine detail). Visibly distinct.
  • Blueprint purpose — a guide for a human tattoo artist. The artist decides shading on skin. The AI does not interpret lighting into fills or dotted shading guides.
  • Readability — clear at thermofax print size, no clutter.

Removed: all fill logic, shadow interpretation, dotted-line shading guides, lighting-density rules, dark-region dotted transitions, crosshatching. Pre-scan is not wired into production.
